# Study identity

Container lengths, tempos, and collections were fixed before note generation. No stochastic draws.

## s01 Downbeat Void

Sixteenth-note rolls leave every quarter-note downbeat empty, accent the offbeat, then thin into long gates on the &.

Limitations: Variation B puts attacks on the downbeat and leaves the & empty. Use kick-b with variation B. Kick-a fits variation A.

## s02 Offbeat Ladder

Odd sixteenths climb and fall through the mode. Bar 4 is half silent. Variation B moves the same color onto the & and rests for a full bar.

Limitations: Variation A bar 4 also sounds on beat 3's downbeat, so a quarter-note kick shares that tick on another channel. The kick reference is one quarter-note pattern for both variations.

## s03 Fifth Gate

Root and fifth alternate on a 3+3+2 sixteenth grid with the bar downbeat left open. Variation B flips the grid to 3+2+3 and treats the fifth as the pedal.

Limitations: Lowest written D in range is D2 (38). D1 is outside the pack range and is not used. One kick marks only the bar downbeat.

## s04 Short Gate Engine

Variation A is a low, mostly stepwise engine with 70-tick gates and a rest on the downbeat of every other bar. Variation B is a five-note loop an octave up, with a whole beat removed every third beat.

Limitations: Kick-a only marks variation A's even-bar downbeats. It does not clear every remaining bass attack. Kick-b marks the beats variation B leaves empty.

## s05 Held Fifth

Multi-bar G and D holds are cut by single-bar bursts. Variation B moves the holds to Ab and Eb and breaks the bursts into fewer attacks.

Limitations: The half-time kick shares time with held bass notes on a different channel. It is a marker, not a mute of the hold.

## s06 Five-Cell Crawl

A five-pitch cell rotates once per bar on a grid that leaves two downbeats empty. Variation B uses a retrograde rotation, the opposite rest grid, and a different departure pitch.

Limitations: Kick-a fits variation A. Kick-b fits variation B. Do not treat either kick as compatible with the other bass variation.

## s07 Question and Gap

Each four-bar cycle asks a denser question, then leaves a long gap with one held C. Variation B replaces the gap with a descending answer on eighth notes.

Limitations: The kick reference is carved to variation A's gaps. Variation B does not keep those gaps.

## s08 Displaced Octave

Syncopated octave leaps sit inside beats 2 and 4. Variation B shifts the grid later by one eighth, swaps octaves for fifths, and inverts which note of the leap is loud.

Limitations: One kick on beats 1 and 3, duration 90 ticks, fits both variations because those bass attacks start after the kick has ended. Variation B does sound inside beats 1 and 3, just not on the tick the kick uses.
